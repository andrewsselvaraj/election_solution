Election Solution - Voter Rules (sample document for the RAG demo)

Voter eligibility
A person may vote if they are a citizen, at least 18 years old on polling day,
and registered on the electoral roll of their constituency.

Documents to bring
Voters must show one photo identity document at the booth: the voter ID card,
a passport, or a driving licence. Photocopies are not accepted.

Mobile phones
Mobile phones, cameras and other recording devices are not allowed inside the
voting compartment. Phones must be switched off and left with the booth staff.

Voters with disabilities
Voters with disabilities may bring one companion aged 18 or over to help them
vote. Ramps and wheelchairs are available at every polling booth.

Queue at closing time
Anyone who is standing in the queue at 18:00 when the booth closes will still
be allowed to vote.

Postal voting
Voters on election duty and voters aged 85 or over may apply for a postal
ballot at least 10 days before polling day.
